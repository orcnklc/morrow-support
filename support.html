Email orcun.kilic@me.com. Include your iOS version and what happened. Please do not send passwords, private messages or payment details.

Protection and permissions

Enable Screen Time permission in iOS Settings, then choose apps and start a pause. Each pause has its own end time. Automatic protection is managed from Automatic pause.

Notifications

Allow notifications in iOS Settings. Focus modes and notification summaries may delay banners. Use the notification test inside the app.

Purchases

Use Restore purchase with the Apple account that purchased Plus or lifetime access. Consumable unlock credits remain on this device and may be lost if the app is deleted or you change devices.

Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account settings. Lifetime access does not automatically cancel an existing subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple at Report a Problem.

Privacy

No separate app account is required. Read how your data stays local.
